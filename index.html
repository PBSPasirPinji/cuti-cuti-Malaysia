Our story

About Jom Cuti

Jom Cuti helps you discover Malaysia's best destinations, plan the perfect itinerary, and travel like a local — from the streets of Penang to the reefs of Perhentian.

"Jom cuti" is Malaysian slang for "let's go on holiday" — and that's exactly the feeling we built this site around. Malaysia packs an enormous amount of variety into one country: hawker food that rivals any capital's fine dining, rainforest older than the Amazon, beaches on two different seas, and a genuine mix of Malay, Chinese, Indian and indigenous Bornean culture in almost every town.

We built Jom Cuti because planning a trip that does all of that justice usually means twenty browser tabs and a half-finished spreadsheet. Instead, we've organised every destination, activity and itinerary idea in one place, and built a planner that turns "I have 5 days and no plan" into a real day-by-day trip in a few clicks.

Locally researched
